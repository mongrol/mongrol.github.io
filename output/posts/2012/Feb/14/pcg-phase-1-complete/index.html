PCG Phase 1 Complete

Published: Tue 14 February 2012
By mongrol

In Traction Edge.

tags: Traction Edge

Phew, that took some doing. For my first PCG map "farm" I've used Cellular Automation for tree generation. This took some tweaking to get right with some random variables liberally sprinkled to get the right amount of clumping and spacing. I didn't want a cavern type thick forest and it was only a couple of integers away from wide open fields with only trees around the edges. Still, I've enough to get on with. I then drawn a road from left to right randomly varying the Y coordinate to make it wander a bit. It's not that clever but good enough to get on with. These coupled with my 5x5 and 10x10 building tiles is making a suitably random farm type map every time.

So, that out of the way I need to go back to progression. I'm going to remove the static content just now while I figure out how to fit in the storyline. I'll change to a simple equip, map, levelup sequence that'll allow me to start looking at balance and more item contents.
