Kung Fu Return

Published: Thu 23 February 2012
By mongrol

In The Skreegs.

tags: Blather

I thought I'd write this down so I don't forget when it happened. After a lapse of nearly 2 1/2 years which has seen me visit various specialists, had numerous scans and a double hip arthroscope, I returned to Kung Fu training last night. The session went well but I expected the worst today since I did have quite sore hip joints. Today though I only have normal muscle soreness from training and no joint pain whatsoever. W00t! I feel my awesomeness returning!
