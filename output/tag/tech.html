I just go icky ficky!

Published: Tue 10 May 2011
By mongrol

In The Skreegs.

tags: Tech

.. I just go, icky ficky ficky!

It's all getting a bit out of control. The mythtv box, which I'm now running tt-rss and roundcube on frequently crashes running out of memory. Turns out it's only ever had 1 GB of ram in it which is pretty good going for a combined back/frontend. I've had to disable apache to keep the WAF. I've ordered a Zotac ND22 for a frontend from PriceUSA (about \$300 delivered) and it's on its way.

I've now got a VPS with Quickweb for serving video's quicker to the folks back home. At some point I'll knock up a basic javascript gallery streaming site on it. As well as host my tt-rss and cube sites. I'm quite impressed with it so far. (lowendscript for teh w1n)

The debian desktop is a tragedy. It throws random segfaults on any heavy process. Much swapping of parts has narrowed it down to the motherboard which I'll now attempt to RMA. Oddly enough one of my replacement parts is a \$40 sempron 140. A single core chip with the mobo has successfully unlocked to dual core. Nice. :)
