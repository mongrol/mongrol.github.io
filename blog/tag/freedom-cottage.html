Woah there slow down!

Published: Tue 19 February 2008
By mongrol

In The Skreegs.

tags: Freedom Cottage

7 days of builders doing stuff so far and they've done a hella lot. Amazing what you can do when someones giving you money to do it. Stuff done so far? Deck ripped up, sealed and nearly tiled, new concrete slab down underneath, round side and behind carport for the tank. Posts and cross timbers in for car port. Frame up for walls and back extension. There's stuff just everywhere. Its like some mad builders construction bomb has went off. Oh, and my water tank finally showed up. No! You can't steal it, I've moved it over the fence.
